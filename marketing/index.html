§ 06 Security

Privacy isn’t a setting.
It’s how the office is built.

Keep the room in the room

Room keys are wrapped to members’ devices. Stored history is ciphertext, and keys rotate when a member leaves.

Belong without a login trail

Join with a passkey or wallet signature. Your office membership doesn’t need an email address or a personal card.

Put guardrails before the send

Browser-side guards check for seed phrases and secrets. Set office rules, approval thresholds, and a clerk kill switch.
